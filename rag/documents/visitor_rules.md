# Visitor Rules

## Section 1 - Visitor Definition

A visitor is any person on campus without a standing staff or student
authorization record. Visitors are expected to be escorted in Tier 2 and
Tier 3 zones at all times.

## Section 2 - Unescorted Visitor Detection

If a detection in a Tier 2 or Tier 3 zone cannot be matched to an escort
record, treat it as an unauthorized entry under the Restricted Area Rules,
not as a routine visitor movement.

## Section 3 - Public Areas

Detections in public or Tier-unclassified areas (lobbies, open walkways,
general parking) do not generate restricted-area incidents regardless of
visitor status. Crowd-formation logic still applies in these areas.
