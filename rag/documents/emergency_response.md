# Emergency Response Steps

## Section 1 - Escalation Triggers

An incident is escalated from a routine security matter to an emergency
when any of the following hold: a Tier 3 zone is entered after hours, a
crowd-formation event exceeds the configured occupancy limit for a space,
or an operator manually flags an incident as an emergency.

## Section 2 - Immediate Actions

On an emergency escalation: notify the on-duty security lead directly
(not just the queue), place the affected zone's live feed at the top of
the dashboard, and hold the incident open until a lead manually closes it
— emergency incidents are never auto-dismissed.

## Section 3 - Crowd Formation Response

When a crowd-formation event is confirmed (occupancy and density both
above the configured threshold for a sustained period), the recommended
action is to notify the facilities and security teams jointly, since crowd
events often require both access control and physical space management.

## Section 4 - After-Action Review

Every emergency-tier incident triggers a mandatory after-action note from
the operator who handled it, recorded against the incident in the report
log.
