# Crowd Management Guidance

## Section 1 - Occupancy Limits

Each monitored space has a configured maximum occupancy. A crowd-formation
event is a candidate when the detected person count exceeds 80% of that
limit for the space.

## Section 2 - Density vs. Count

Person count alone can be misleading in large open areas; density
(people per unit area) and motion score are used alongside count to avoid
flagging normal foot traffic as a crowd event. A sustained high-density,
low-motion reading (people gathered and stationary) is treated as a
stronger signal than a high-count, high-motion reading (people passing
through).

## Section 3 - Recommended Response

For a confirmed crowd event below the hard occupancy limit, the
recommended action is a monitoring advisory to facilities staff. For a
crowd event that reaches or exceeds the hard occupancy limit, the
recommended action escalates to the emergency response procedure for
crowd formation.
